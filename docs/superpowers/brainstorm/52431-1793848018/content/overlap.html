When the names reach the scene

The real sidebar: Explorer at the top, Worktrees always holding at least the bottom third. Each option is shown twice: with a short Worktrees list, which leaves room at the foot, and with a long one, which doesn't.

room at the foot
a long list

1. Shrink, then hide

The scene fills only the space below the last name. With room, the ground, pines and castle show and the sky is cut short. As the list grows the scene is cut from the top, ground last, and below about 50 points it is gone. Text never sits on art.

room at the foot
a long list

2. Stay, dimmed under text

The scene always fills the foot of the sidebar. Behind each line of names it fades almost to the plain sidebar colour, and it shows in full only where no name is. With a long list most of it is a faint ghost.
