A, calmer: two levels

Both keep the art away from anything you read. No stars behind file names or tab labels, a plain tab bar, and the landscape only at the foot of the sidebar. The second goes further and dims the art toward the theme's own purples, so it reads like a painted backdrop.

A1. Soft (my pick)

The full colours, but only where nothing is written: a few stars at the title bar's ends and in the sidebar's empty sky, the castle, pines and grass at the bottom. The tab bar is the theme's own, with a small lit lantern on the active tab. Moving: stars twinkle, castle windows flicker, fireflies rise; nothing moves behind text.

A2. Softer

The same scene, its colours pulled toward the theme's purples: the grass a violet green, the castle a silhouette with only its windows lit. Calmer still, at the cost of some of the Game Boy brightness.
