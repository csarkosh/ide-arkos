The states, and where each comes from

SectionMail / Slack habitMeansArkos knows it from
Needs youSlack's red badge: a mentionClaude is blocked on a permission or a question. Nothing moves until you act.The permission hook. Today it is merged with "stopped"; splitting it needs a new hook entry (the doctor would offer it).
UnreadBold in the inboxClaude finished its turn and you have not opened it since.The Stop hook, until the session is shown and focused.
UnansweredRead, not replied toYou saw the result and have not sent anything. Still your move, but no longer new.New: today a click clears the dot and the session looks idle.
WorkingSentYou replied; Claude is working. Nothing to do.The prompt and tool hooks, as today.
DoneArchiveYou marked it done, or its shell ended. Folded away; opening one resumes it.New: a mark you set. Resume uses the record Arkos already keeps for recovery.

The line under each name is Claude's last message, or what it is asking. Arkos is already told where each session's transcript is and does not read it yet.

How far to go

No new layout yet.

2 · The session-first layout my pick

Step 1, plus the layout above.

  • Layout: Grid or the new one, in Settings

  • Mark unread, Done

3 · An inbox across projects

Step 2, plus:

  • Sessions of every open project in one list
  • Later (snooze until a time)
  • Ended sessions kept and searchable, resumed on open
  • A macOS notification for Needs you

The name

Inbox my pickSays what it does in one word: sessions sorted by what needs you. Everyone knows how an inbox behaves.
ThreadsWhat Codex and Slack call the same thing. Describes the list, not the triage.
DeskFriendlier, less exact.
JournalReads as a dated record you look back through, which fits step 3's kept history more than the attention list. Arkos also already has two things called a journal: the update log and the usage journal.
