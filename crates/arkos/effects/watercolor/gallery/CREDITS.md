# The Watercolor gallery

Pane 0 under the Watercolor effect shows these works one at a time, through the effect's
watercolour filter. Every one is in the public domain and released by its museum under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0); each file here is the museum's image shrunk to 384 points on
its long side.
