# Trip notes

- Lisbon, two of us, Monday November 3 to Saturday November 8.
- Flights booked: arrive Monday 09:40 (LIS), depart Saturday 17:05. Nothing else booked.
- Hotel booked, Baixa, four nights paid already (not part of the budget below). Checkout is
  Saturday morning, so Saturday needs a plan for the bags.
- Budget for everything else: 2,400 USD total for both of us (food, transport, tickets, the
  day trip, small shopping). Prefer to come in under.
- Wants: food markets and small lunch places over fancy dinners; the tile museum; one day in
  Sintra (Pena and the Quinta da Regaleira, not the whole list); Belém for the monastery and
  the pastéis; a sunset miradouro at least twice; one evening of fado if it is not a tourist
  trap.
- Avoid: a rental car; anything that needs booking weeks ahead that we have not booked;
  back-to-back museum days (M. gets tired by mid-afternoon; plan a long lunch and a slow
  afternoon each day).
- Transport: fine with metro, trams and walking; happy to take the train to Sintra.
- Weather in November: mild, some rain; pack for 12 to 18 C.
