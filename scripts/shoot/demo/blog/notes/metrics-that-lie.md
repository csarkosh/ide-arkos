# Notes toward a post: metrics that lie

- the weekly active number went up 30% in March; it was the new onboarding email sending
  everyone back to the app once to dismiss a banner
- "time in app" rewarded the slowest screens
- support tickets per user fell when we made the contact form harder to find (we fixed that)
- the honest metric was boring: how many teams ran the weekly report two weeks in a row
- rule I want to propose: before you celebrate a metric, name one way it could have moved
  for a bad reason, and check that first
- a story about the meeting where someone asked "did anything else change that week" and
  the room went quiet
- end on the boring metric and why I like boring
