# What I learned shipping a settings page

Nobody wants to own settings. That is exactly why they matter.

## The brief

Twelve toggles, three of which nobody could explain. Support tickets about two of them every
week. A design that had been "almost done" since spring.

## What we changed

We cut the twelve to seven and wrote one sentence under each that says what happens when you
flip it. Not what the feature is: what happens. "Weekly report goes to everyone on the team"
beats "Enable team digest."

## What I would do again

Read the support tickets first. Ship the copy change a week before the layout change, so we
could tell which one moved the numbers. (It was the copy.)
