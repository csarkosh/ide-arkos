# Saying no in writing

The hardest part of the job is not deciding. It is writing the decision down where the people
who disagree can read it.

## The template I use

What was asked. What we are doing instead, and when we will look again. The reason, in one
paragraph, without blaming the asker. Who to talk to if this is wrong.

## Why writing beats a meeting

A meeting ends and everyone remembers a different version. A paragraph is the same for
everyone, including the person who joins next month and asks why we never built the thing.

## The part that still feels bad

It never stops feeling like a small betrayal. I have decided that is the correct amount of
discomfort for telling someone no.
