# The one-pager that saved a quarter

Every project I have regretted started with a deck. Every one I am proud of started with a
page.

## What goes on the page

The problem in two sentences, written as the customer would say it. The one metric that
moves if we are right. What we will not do. Who decides what, and by when.

## Why it works

A page has no room for hedging. A deck lets six people each add a slide and nobody own the
whole. The page forces a single author, and a single author can be wrong in a way you can
point at.

## The habit

Write the page before the kickoff, not after. Read it aloud to one engineer and one person
from support. If either of them frowns, the page is not done.
