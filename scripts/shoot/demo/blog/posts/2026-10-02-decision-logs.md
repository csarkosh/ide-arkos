# Keep a decision log, not a changelog

A changelog says what changed. A decision log says why, and the why is what you need a year
later.

## The format

One line a decision: the date, the decision, the alternative we passed on, and the one reason
that tipped it. If the reason takes more than a sentence, the decision was not clear yet.

## Where it lives

In the repository, next to the code, in a file anyone can edit. Not in a wiki nobody opens.
The engineers read it because it is in the way, which is the point.

## What changed for us

Arguments got shorter. Half of them turned out to be the same argument we had already had,
and the log said so.
